Fair Play

Every round uses a secret seed created with the server's cryptographically secure generator (PHP random_int / random_bytes), never browser Math.random(). The wheel animation is cosmetic only and does not affect the outcome.

  • A SHA-256 commitment to the outcome is shown before you pick.
  • The outcome is decided on the server and stored before you pick; your browser never knows it in advance.
  • After the reveal the committed data is shown, so you can re-hash it and confirm it was not changed.
  • Odds are configurable and validated to never exceed 100%.
  • Each round has a unique game ID and timestamp recorded in the play history.
